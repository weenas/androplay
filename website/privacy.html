隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

映湾（CastBay）是一款运行在 Android TV 上的开源投屏接收 App。我们重视你的隐私：映湾不需要账号，不含广告，也不包含任何统计或追踪代码。

我们不收集的信息

映湾不会收集、存储或上传你的个人信息，也不会把你的使用情况发送给我们或任何第三方。我们没有服务器来接收这类数据。

投屏内容

  • 通过 AirPlay 投屏时，画面和声音只在你的手机与电视之间、在你的局域网内传输。
  • 通过 App 投送视频或音乐时（AirPlay 视频或 DLNA），电视会直接从该 App 提供的地址下载并播放内容。这与在电视上直接观看该内容相同，相关的数据处理由提供内容的服务负责。
  • 映湾在局域网内发布设备名称，以便你的手机能找到它。

歌词（可选功能）

"显示歌词"默认关闭。开启后，播放音乐时映湾会把歌曲的名称、歌手、专辑和时长发送到 lrclib.net，用于查询歌词。请求中不包含任何可以识别你身份的信息。该网站的数据处理适用其自身的政策。你可以随时在设置或播放时的快捷菜单中关闭此功能。

设置和本机数据

你的设置（例如设备名称、投屏密码）只保存在电视本机，不会上传。卸载映湾会一并删除这些数据。

权限说明

  • 网络：接收投屏、播放 App 投送的内容。
  • 位置（仅在你主动点击"显示 Wi-Fi 名称"时申请）：Android 规定只有获得位置权限的 App 才能读取 Wi-Fi 名称。映湾只用它在主界面显示网络名称，不会读取或使用你的位置。
  • 开机启动、前台服务：让映湾在电视开机后自动就绪，并在后台继续播放。

儿童隐私

映湾不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更与联系方式

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新此页面及生效日期。如有问题，请在 GitHub 上提交问题。

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 26, 2026

CastBay is an open-source casting receiver app for Android TV. It needs no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking code.

What we don't collect

CastBay does not collect, store or upload personal information, and does not send your usage to us or anyone else. We have no server to receive such data.

What you cast

  • AirPlay mirroring and music travel only between your phone and TV, on your local network.
  • When an app casts a video or song (AirPlay video or DLNA), the TV fetches it directly from the address that app provides, just as if you watched it on the TV. The service providing the content is responsible for its data handling.
  • CastBay announces its device name on your local network so your phone can find it.

Lyrics (optional)

"Show lyrics" is off by default. When you turn it on, CastBay sends the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net to look up its lyrics. Nothing that identifies you is included, and that site's own policy applies to it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings or the quick menu while playing.

Settings and data on the TV

Your settings (such as the device name and casting password) stay on the TV and are never uploaded. Uninstalling CastBay deletes them.

Permissions

  • Network: to receive casts and play what apps cast.
  • Location, requested only when you choose "Show Wi-Fi name": Android only lets apps with this permission read the Wi-Fi name. CastBay uses it to show the network's name on the home screen and never reads or uses your location.
  • Start at boot and foreground service: so CastBay is ready when the TV turns on and keeps playing in the background.

Children's privacy

CastBay collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
